## About Shiftchi

Shiftchi is a smart application for managing work shifts. It helps users easily record and manage their shifts and sync them with Google Calendar.

🌐 Website: [shiftchi.fuladpanjeh.ir](https://shiftchi.fuladpanjeh.ir/)

### Features

Shiftchi
- Modern user interface
- Data management
- Image and PDF export
- Notifications
- Google Calendar sync

Shift Management
- Record shifts, leave, and overtime
- Voice and text notes
- Food meals and custom items
- Solar calendar
- Statistics and reporting

Break Management
- Shift break management

Payroll Management
- Record pay
- Pay comparison
- Currency conversion
- Reporting

Quality Assurance (QA)
- Quality score recording
- Reporting

Key Performance Indicators (KPI)
- Performance indicators
- Reporting

### Built With
- **Flutter** — Cross-platform mobile app framework
- **Google Sign-In** — Secure authentication for Google Calendar integration
